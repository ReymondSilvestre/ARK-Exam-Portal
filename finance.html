Payment computation - total to pay per student

Calculated separately for each voucher type (PUB, ALS, ESC and so on) from the fee schedule in Settings, and for each grade level on the enrolled masterlist. Below each amount: the fees that make it up and how many enrolled students it applies to.

History log

Every payment recorded or voided, and every change to the payment settings, with who did it and when. Entries cannot be edited or deleted.

Payment transactions

The kinds of payment the school collects, for example Tuition, Miscellaneous and Intramurals. Each one can be given an amount per grade level and voucher type in the fee schedule, and is chosen when a payment is recorded.

Fee schedule -

Pick a voucher group, then type the amount each grade level pays for each transaction. A student's total to pay uses the group of his or her voucher type. A blank cell falls back to Default for all students; type 0 to make it free for that group.

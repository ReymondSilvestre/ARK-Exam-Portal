Sections

Every section has its own classroom code. Give it to students of other teachers in your school: they join from their account and appear under that section (their own section and teacher stay separate). They then see your exam results and modules.

Students

Student logins

Username = Exam ID No. Temporary password = birth year + first letter of the last name (example 2012D). The student must choose a new password at first sign-in. If you entered the student's real email, the login is created with that email: the student can still sign in with the Exam ID No. Enter the email before creating the login. Password resets are done only by the school's IT Admin.

Answer sheets

Paper: 8.5 x 13 in, landscape (Folio / Long bond), two answer sheets per page. In the print dialog choose that paper size, Margins: None and Scale: 100%. Cut each page along the dotted line: scan one sheet at a time. Named sheets are printed in alphabetical order for the chosen section.

Scan answer sheets

Start the camera and hold ONE answer sheet in view, upright, with all 4 black corner squares visible. It is detected automatically and photographed as soon as it is steady.

Sections

Every section has its own classroom code. Give it to students of other teachers in your school: they join from their account and appear under that section (their own section and teacher stay separate). They then see your exam results and modules.

Students

Student logins

Username = Exam ID No. Temporary password = birth year + first letter of the last name (example 2012D). The student must choose a new password at first sign-in.

New examination

Item types and answer key

Tap a number to open it. Multiple choice and True/False are scanned automatically. Identification and essay are checked by you after scanning.

Upload a module

Students see modules for their own section (or for all your sections), organised by term / quarter.

Answer sheets

Paper: 8.5 x 13 in, landscape (Folio / Long bond), two answer sheets per page. In the print dialog choose that paper size, Margins: None and Scale: 100%. Cut each page along the dotted line: scan one sheet at a time. Named sheets are printed in alphabetical order for the chosen section.
